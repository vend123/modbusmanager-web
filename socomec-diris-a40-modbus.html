Socomec DIRIS A-40 Modbus: Two Register Maps, Two Scalings, THD and Harmonics

The Socomec DIRIS A-40 is a panel-mounted multifunction meter and power analyser — RS-485 (48250500) or with an Ethernet gateway (48250501). Its Modbus table holds the same measurements twice: a legacy block at 50512 that older DIRIS integrations use, and a newer layout from 18432 and 36864. They are not scaled the same way, and mixing them is the classic DIRIS mistake. This guide covers both, plus the THD and individual harmonic registers.

Connection and Addressing

  • Function codes: FC03 to read, FC06 / FC16 to write. Everything is an integer (U16, S16, U32, S32, U64) — no floats.
  • Addresses as listed: Socomec gives each address in decimal and hex (50512 = 0xC550); that number goes in the request. No 3xxxx / 4xxxx notation.
  • Serial settings are set on the meter and can be read back over Modbus: 57344 slave address, 57345 baud (3 = 9600, 4 = 19200, 5 = 38400, 6 = 57600, 7 = 115200), 57346 stop bits (0 = 1, 1 = 2), 57347 parity (0 none, 1 odd, 2 even). The Ethernet variant has its network settings from 29184.
  • Identify it: 50000–50003 read “SOCO”; 50006 is the Modbus table version, 50007 the software version (100 = 1.00).
DIRIS A-40, not DIRIS A40. The older DIRIS A40 / A41 (no hyphen) shares the legacy 50512 block but not the newer ranges, and its manuals are a different document. Check the label before using a register list from the web.

THD of voltage lives in the network range (371xx), THD of current in the load range (187xx). A value of 1420 is 14.20 %. Register 34819 says whether THD is referred to the fundamental (0) or to the RMS value (1).

Common Mistakes

  • Power ten times too small or too big: legacy 50536 is in 10 W, 18476 in W.
  • Frequency 49 990 Hz: 36871 is mHz; legacy 50526 is Hz / 100.
  • Current 84 600 A: the currents are in mA.
  • Energy that resets: 50780 is the partial counter; the total is 19843.
  • THD 1420 %: the / 100 not applied.
  • A negative power factor read as export: the sign of PF says leading or lagging, not the direction of power.
  • A register list for “DIRIS A40” from a forum: another product generation — some copies even give float registers the A-40 does not have.

Step-by-Step: Reading a DIRIS A-40 with ModbusManager

  1. Set the meter's address, baud rate and parity in its menu (or use the Ethernet variant on port 502).
  2. In ModbusManager choose RTU with those settings, or TCP with the gateway's address.
  3. Read FC03 from 50512, quantity 62: voltages, currents in mA, power in 10 W, power factor ÷ 1000.
  4. Read 37144, quantity 8, and 18757, quantity 4, for THD ÷ 100. Or open the workspace below.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Modbus register gives total active power on a DIRIS A-40?

Legacy 50512 block: 50536, signed 32-bit in 10 W (5270 = 52.7 kW). Newer layout: 18476, signed 32-bit in W. Both are read with FC03, address as listed.

Why is the DIRIS A-40 frequency 49990?

You are reading 36871 in the newer layout, which is in mHz. The legacy register 50526 gives the frequency in Hz / 100 (4999).

Where are THD registers on a Socomec DIRIS A-40?

THD of voltage at 37144–37151 (V1–V3, U12–U31, system), THD of current at 18757–18760 (I1–I3, In) and 18767 (system), all U16 in % / 100. Individual harmonics start at 37504 (voltage) and 19713 (current).

Does the DIRIS A-40 use float registers?

No. Socomec's table lists only integers (U16, S16, U32, S32, U64). Register lists with floats belong to other devices or other generations.

What are the default serial settings of a DIRIS A-40?

Socomec's Modbus table does not state factory defaults. They are set in the meter's menu and can be read back at 57344 (address), 57345 (baud), 57346 (stop bits) and 57347 (parity).

Sources
