GoodWe ET Hybrid Inverter Modbus: Address 247 and the 35100 Block

GoodWe's hybrid inverters — ET, EH, BT, BH and their relatives — publish a Modbus map that most integrations use without reading the first page. That page says the inverter answers on address 247, not 1. This guide covers the connection settings, the live-data block from 35100, the battery and BMS registers and a sign question the document leaves open, from GoodWe's own Modbus protocol for energy storage inverters.

Connection Settings

SettingValue
ProtocolModbus RTU on RS485
Inverter address1–247, factory default 247 (0xF7)
Baud rate9600 (default)
Byte format1 start, 8 data, 1 stop — no parity (8N1)
Function codes03 read, 06 write single, 10h write multiple
Errors02 register address fault or too many registers, 03 data error, 04 verification error

A master that polls address 1 gets silence and usually reports a timeout, not a wrong address — the most common reason a GoodWe "does not support Modbus". Values are integers sent high byte first; 32-bit values come in two registers, high word first.

Meter values sit from 36000: 36004 meter communication status (1 OK), 36005–36008 phase and total active power in 16 bits, and from ARM firmware above version 9 the 32-bit set from 36019, with total active power at 36025.

Which Way Does the Power Flow?

GoodWe's protocol gives battery power (35182) and meter power as signed numbers, but it does not say which sign means charging or exporting. Do not guess from one reading: 35184 Battery Mode says it in words — 2 discharging, 3 charging — and grid power can be calculated from the balance, load − PV − battery discharge. The workspace's script does both, so the flow diagram is right whatever sign your firmware uses.

Common Mistakes

  • Timeout on every request: polling address 1; the factory address is 247.
  • Parity errors: the inverter uses 8N1, not 8E1.
  • PV voltage 4126: divide by 10; frequency by 100.
  • Exception 02: a register outside the map, or too many registers in one request.
  • Battery arrow the wrong way: the sign of 35182 is not documented; use 35184.
  • FC04 refused: GoodWe reads with FC03.

Step-by-Step: Reading a GoodWe ET with ModbusManager

  1. Wire a USB–RS485 adapter to the inverter's RS485 / COM port (or use a Modbus TCP gateway).
  2. In ModbusManager choose RTU, 9600, no parity, 1 stop bit, unit 247.
  3. Poll FC03 from 35103, quantity 8: PV1 and PV2 voltage, current and power.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the default Modbus address of a GoodWe inverter?

247 (0xF7). GoodWe's protocol allows 1 to 247 and ships with 247, so a master polling address 1 gets no answer. The default baud rate is 9600, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit.

Where is PV power in the GoodWe Modbus map?

PV1 power is 35105 (U32, W), PV2 35109, PV3 35113, PV4 35117. Voltages are at 35103, 35107 and so on, divided by 10.

How do I read the GoodWe battery state of charge?

Register 37007 holds the BMS state of charge in percent. Battery voltage is 35180 (/10), current 35181 (/10), power 35182 and the charge / discharge state 35184.

Is GoodWe battery power positive when charging?

The protocol does not say. Use Battery1 Mode 35184 for the direction (2 discharging, 3 charging) and the magnitude of 35182 for the power.

Which function code does GoodWe use?

FC03 to read, FC06 to write a single register and FC10 (16) to write several. 32-bit values come high word first.
