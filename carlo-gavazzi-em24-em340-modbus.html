Carlo Gavazzi EM340 and EM24 Modbus: Registers, Scaling, Word Order

Carlo Gavazzi meters — the EM340 on RS-485 and the EM24 with Ethernet are among the most common — share one register map and one set of habits: values are integers with a fixed weight (volts × 10, amps × 1000), 32-bit values come low word first, and FC03 and FC04 return the same thing. This page covers the map, the scaling and the settings from Carlo Gavazzi's own protocol documents, plus a register that changes meaning depending on how you read it.

On the EM24 E1, IP changes written to 2100h–210Dh only apply after writing 1 to 210Eh (apply TCP/IP changes). Two stop bits on the EM340 are only possible with even parity.

Addresses: Modicom and Physical

Gavazzi lists every value twice: a six-digit "Modicom" address such as 300001 and a "physical" address such as 0000h — the physical one is what goes in the frame. Physical = Modicom − 300001. With FC03 the Modicom address starts with 4 instead of 3, and FC03 and FC04 have exactly the same effect: use whichever your master prefers.

Low word first

"In INT32, UINT32 and UINT64 formats, the word order is LSW → MSW." V L1-N at 230.5 V is 2305 = 0x00000901, sent as 0901 0000. Read high word first and you get 151 060 480 — or, more often, a voltage that looks fine at first and jumps wildly as soon as the value passes 6553.5. Most Modbus tools call this "word swap", "little-endian words" or "CDAB".

An overflowing value (display "EEE") reads FFFF 7FFF in the frame — 0x7FFFFFFF.

One address, two meanings: 000Bh

000Bh is the high word of V L3-L1 (000Ah–000Bh) — and also the meter's identification code, which the protocol says is read "limited to a word at a time": 341 for an EM340, 345 for an ET340, 332 for an EM330. A one-word read of 000Bh returns the code; a block read across 000Bh returns the voltage word. If your master merges requests, the code can come back as 0. The workspace reads it alone, with FC03.

Easy connection or bidirectional

Models ending PF A count in "easy connection" mode: current and power always positive, kWh always increasing whatever the direction — the right choice when a CT may be fitted backwards. PF B models are bidirectional: negative power is export and goes to kWh (−) at 004Eh. On a PV or battery circuit you need the PF B variant.

Common Mistakes

  • Values in the millions: INT32 read high word first. The EM340 sends the low word first.
  • 230.5 V reads 2305: the weight; divide by 10. Current is × 1000, so 14200 is 14.2 A.
  • Power always positive on a PV feed: a PF A meter in easy connection mode.
  • ID code reads 0: 000Bh read inside a block; read it as a single word.
  • Reflections on a long line: no A− to T bridge on the last meter.
  • EM24 E1 IP change ignored: 210Eh (apply) was not written.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the word order of Carlo Gavazzi EM340 registers?

Low word first. The protocol says INT32, UINT32 and UINT64 values are sent LSW to MSW, with each word big-endian — often called word swap or CDAB.

How are EM340 values scaled?

They are integers with a fixed weight: voltage × 10, current × 1000, power × 10 (W), power factor × 1000, frequency × 10 and energy × 10 (kWh).

Should I use FC03 or FC04 with Carlo Gavazzi meters?

Either: the protocol says functions 03h and 04h have exactly the same effect. The physical address is the Modicom address minus 300001, so V L1-N 300001 is address 0.

What is the default IP address of the Carlo Gavazzi EM24 Ethernet?

The EM24-DIN E1 is set to 192.168.1.10, mask 255.255.255.0, gateway 192.168.1.1, DHCP off, Modbus TCP port 502. After changing them, write 1 to register 210Eh to apply.

Why does the EM340 identification code read 0?

Address 000Bh is also the high word of V L3-L1. The code (341 for an EM340) is returned only when 000Bh is read as a single word, not inside a block.
